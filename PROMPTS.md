# PROMPTS.md — AI usage log

Add at least five dated entries during your actual project work. Do not claim work you did not perform.

For each entry record:
- Date
- Prompt or fair summary of the prompt
- What the tool produced
- What was wrong or incomplete
- What you changed yourself
- Link to the commit containing your fix

At least two entries must discuss authorization, access control or the audit trail.
